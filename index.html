X0X Live for M-VAVE FM-1

An alternative FM-1 firmware for making acid music as you play. Discover a bassline, keep an idea you like, develop it, bring in drums and direct the next break or drop. Two independent 303s, 909 and 808 drums, hands-on sound controls and performance effects share one LIVE interface.

Current version: 1.2.1-live9 · experimental. Hardware validation is pending.

Play in your browser · Install on FM-1 · Full controls guide · Download firmware

What it does

  • Two acid voices. Independent 303 patches with cutoff, resonance, envelope, decay, accent, waveform and drive, plus pan and effect sends.
  • Generate, keep, develop. Discover phrases, LIKE an idea, explore variations and RETURN to it. Musical undo keeps your later sound changes.
  • Generative drums. 909 and 808 kits with eleven instruments each. Weird and Full move a groove from sparse and straight to busy and adventurous. Kick and hats start audible; bring in other instruments with the keys.
  • Direct the performance. Queue INTRO, GROOVE, BUILD, BREAK, BRIDGE, DROP or OUTRO. Extend or shorten the current segment by a bar. The Director prototype can prepare a complementary 303 B phrase and coordinated drums around A.
  • Play the mix. Direct part and drum mutes, solo, linked selection and mix restoration. Four controls always follow the physical K1–K4 order.
  • Shape movement and space. Bar-aligned modulation, growing and falling ramps; delay and reverb controls; momentary filters, pump, drive, tape stop, sixteenth-note chopper and captured audio repeats.

Install

  1. Back up first. For Sloop, use the Sloop editor. The X0X backup page handles X0X projects only. Keep your previous firmware and its recovery instructions.
  2. Connect the FM-1 with a USB data cable. Close other MIDI apps and editor tabs.
  3. Open the web installer in desktop Chrome or Edge and allow MIDI / SysEx access when asked.
  4. Follow the connection and installation instructions. Keep USB connected until the update completes and the instrument restarts.
  5. Start playing on LIVE.

Direct switching from every alternative firmware, including Sloop, is not yet verified. If the instrument will not start, see the recovery instructions.

Your first groove

  1. Start with 303 A selected. Press OCT+ to generate a phrase, then PLAY.
  2. Turn K1–K4 for cutoff, resonance, envelope modulation and decay.
  3. Tap ARP for Director, then ARP again to request a related B phrase and drum groove. During playback it arrives at the next segment boundary.
  4. Press SEQ to LIKE selected musical material. Use OCT− to develop it; GLO returns to the capture. REC undoes the last musical change.
  5. Tap LFO for Mixer. Black keys mute/unmute parts; white keys mute/unmute individual drums. PRESETS switches the focused 909/808 kit.
  6. Tap FX, then hold white keys for momentary effects. Release to return.

Find your controls

ButtonWhat you get
EDITSelected instrument's sound; tap again for extra controls
LFOMixer; repeated taps cycle Level, Pan, Reverb send, Delay send
FXMomentary, Delay and Reverb views; repeated taps cycle them
ENVAutomation: destination, depth, bars and shape
ARPDirector: Weird, Full, segment length and next section
HOMEReturn to LIVE sound controls
SAVESave the current project

SELECT also cycles views inside Mixer and FX. HOME + SELECT changes tempo there; SELECT elsewhere in LIVE changes tempo directly.

The first four black keys select 909 / 808 / 303 A / 303 B outside Mixer; hold SEL to select several. On a drum sound screen, white keys select the instrument named in the heading. In Mixer, those keys operate mutes instead. Hold REC + a musical key for solo. HOME + SEL restores the captured mix.

Global FX controls: Delay has time division, feedback, tone and return level. Reverb has decay, tone, low cut and return level. White keys keep their momentary FX gestures in all three FX views.

Automation: K2 sets depth from 0 to 127. White keys offer three depths each of modulation, growing and falling ramps; cycles are 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars. A four-bar ramp engaged in bar 3 joins the current phase and resets after bar 4. Turn the sound knob to take over manually.

For detailed editing, hold SEL + SEQ; for settings, SEL + GLO. Press HOME to come back. The controls guide covers every key gesture, section control and automation preset.

Saving and current limits

SAVE preserves the project. LIKE captures patterns and generator settings, not synth patches or the mix. Captures, musical undo, manual mutes, section position and automation are temporary performance state and do not survive a restart. Named scene presets and a persistent favorites library are not available yet.

Performance audio effects currently act on the master mix. Repeat history is about 371 ms, so longer repeats are capped. Maximum delay is about 1.26 seconds. Breakbeat playback is disabled in this version. The Director is an early prototype; the firmware has not yet been validated on physical FM-1 hardware.
